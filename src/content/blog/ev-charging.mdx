# Pakistan’s Auto Sector Accelerates EV Shift Amid Rising Fuel Costs and Regional Uncertainty

Pakistan’s automobile industry is witnessing renewed momentum in electric and hybrid mobility as rising fuel prices and regional instability reshape consumer behavior. Local assemblers and investors are moving toward electric bikes, hybrid SUVs, and compact EVs.

The latest developments include possible electric bike production by a major tractor manufacturer and new hybrid and electric vehicle plans from Chery Master Pakistan.

---

## Electric Bike Demand Surges Across Pakistan

Electric bikes and scooters are experiencing strong demand due to rising petrol prices. Consumers in urban areas are increasingly shifting toward EV alternatives because of lower running costs.

Sales of electric bikes and scooters increased significantly during March and April, especially in major cities where commuting costs are high.

Supply shortages have emerged in some models, leading to price premiums of around Rs10,000 to Rs15,000 in certain markets.

### Supply Chain Response

Manufacturers have increased imports of parts and accessories to meet rising demand by opening additional letters of credit.

This indicates expectations that EV demand will remain strong in the short term, although demand may stabilize if fuel prices fall.

---

## Millat Group Evaluates Electric Bike Production

A major tractor manufacturer group, Millat Tractors Limited, is evaluating the feasibility of entering the electric bike segment through one of its associated companies.

The initiative is still in the early feasibility stage and no final decision has been made.

### Strategic Importance

If implemented, this move could bring:

- Strong manufacturing capacity into the EV bike market  
- Established supply chain infrastructure  
- Potential for lower-cost local EV production  
- Increased competition in the electric two-wheeler sector  

---

## Chery Master Pakistan Expands EV and Hybrid Plans

Chery Master Pakistan is considering the introduction of new energy vehicles for the local market, following its participation in Auto China 2026.

Planned models include:

- Tiggo 4 HEV  
- QQ BEV  
- Tiggo V  

These vehicles reflect a broader shift toward hybrid and electric mobility solutions in Pakistan.

---

## Compact EV Focus for Urban Markets

The QQ BEV is a compact electric vehicle designed for urban environments.

### Key Characteristics

- Estimated range: up to 410 km  
- Designed for city driving  
- Lower operating cost compared to petrol vehicles  

Urban centers such as Karachi, Lahore, and Islamabad are key target markets due to traffic congestion and fuel cost pressures.

---

## Expansion of Hybrid SUV Lineup

Chery Master Pakistan has already introduced plug-in hybrid SUVs in Pakistan, including:

- Tiggo 9 PHEV  
- Tiggo 8 PHEV  
- Tiggo 7 PHEV  

These models combine fuel efficiency with reduced dependence on charging infrastructure.

### Why Hybrids Are Growing

- Lower fuel consumption  
- Reduced dependency on petrol  
- No need for extensive charging infrastructure  
- Better suitability for mixed driving conditions  

---

## Challenges in Pakistan’s EV Ecosystem

### Limited Charging Infrastructure

Charging stations remain limited outside major cities, restricting long-distance EV adoption.

### Import Dependency

The sector depends heavily on imported batteries, motors, and electronic components.

### Policy and Stability Issues

Long-term growth depends on stable policies, incentives, and infrastructure development.

---

## Market Outlook

Pakistan’s auto industry is shifting toward electric and hybrid mobility due to:

- Rising fuel prices  
- Urban congestion  
- Increasing consumer awareness  
- Global EV adoption trends  

While infrastructure gaps remain, the market direction suggests accelerated EV adoption in the coming years.
